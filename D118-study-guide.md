## Evidence-Based Practice

### Question 1

**Which study design provides the highest level of evidence for clinical decision-making?**

1. [ ] Case-control study
2. [ ] Cohort study
3. [✓] Randomized controlled trial
4. [ ] Cross-sectional study

**Explanation:** RCTs are the gold standard — randomization controls for confounders and establishes causation.

---

### Question 2

**A patient asks why you're using a specific treatment. You cite a systematic review of RCTs. What USPSTF grade would indicate high certainty of substantial benefit?**

1. [ ] Grade B
2. [✓] Grade A
3. [ ] Grade C
4. [ ] Grade D

**Explanation:** Grade A = high certainty of substantial net benefit. Grade B = high certainty of moderate benefit.

### Question 1

**A patient with sudden dyspnea, pleuritic chest pain, tachycardia, and recent long flight. What is the FIRST diagnostic test?**

1. [ ] V/Q scan
2. [ ] CT pulmonary angiography (CTPA)
3. [✓] D-dimer if low pretest probability

**Explanation:** Use Wells criteria first. If low pretest probability → D-dimer. If elevated → CTPA. CTPA is gold standard but D-dimer can rule out in low-probability patients.
