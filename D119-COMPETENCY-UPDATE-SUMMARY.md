Based on the D119 Notetaking Guide structure, identified these major competency areas:

#### **PRIMARY COMPETENCY AREAS:**

1. **Developmental Milestones (Units 2, 3, 4)** — ~25%
   - Newborn assessment (APGAR, reflexes, physical exam)
   - Infant/toddler milestones (2-4-6-9-12-18-24 months)
   - Preschool development (3-5 years)
   - Middle childhood (6-12 years)
   - Adolescence (puberty, Tanner staging, psychosocial development)
   - Developmental screening tools (ASQ-3, BINS, M-CHAT, Vanderbilt)
   - Red flags and referral criteria

2. **Conceptualizing Healthy Behaviors** — ~12%
   - Environmental hazards (lead, tobacco, CO, mercury, pesticides)
   - Injury prevention (intentional/unintentional)
   - Child maltreatment (physical, sexual, psychological, neglect)
   - Nutrition (breastfeeding, formula, solid foods, obesity, food allergies)
   - Elimination patterns (bowel and bladder dysfunction)

3. **Self-Perception, Relationships & Sexuality** — ~8%
   - Protective factors (strengthening families framework)
   - Developmental stages of sexuality (infancy through adolescence)
   - Sexual health history and physical examination
   - LGBTQ+ youth considerations
   - Shared decision-making (Three Talk Model)

4. **Mental Health & Behavioral Disorders** — ~15%
   - ADHD (DSM-5 criteria, screening, management)
   - Anxiety disorders (GAD, SAD, OCD, social anxiety)
   - Depression and bipolar disorder
   - Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)
   - Learning disorders (Specific Learning Disorder)
   - Conduct disorder and Oppositional Defiant Disorder
   - Eating disorders
   - Substance abuse disorders
   - Screening tools (PHQ-2, PHQ-9, SCARED, STAIC, PSC)

5. **Infectious Diseases** — ~20%
   - Normal microbiota and pathogen transmission
   - Common childhood infections by system
   - Viral exanthems (measles, mumps, rubella, roseola, fifth disease, varicella)
   - Respiratory infections (RSV, croup, pneumonia, strep pharyngitis)
   - Vector-borne diseases (Lyme, RMSF, West Nile)
   - Immunizations (schedule, contraindications, adverse reactions)
   - HIV, TB, meningococcal disease
   - Fever management by age

6. **System-Based Pediatric Conditions** — ~10%
   - Cardiovascular (innocent murmurs, congenital heart disease)
   - Respiratory (asthma, bronchiolitis, croup)
   - Gastrointestinal (constipation, intussusception, GERD)
   - Genitourinary (UTI, enuresis, hypospadias, hydrocele)
   - Neurological (seizures, headaches, Bell's palsy, meningitis)
   - Dermatology (eczema, common rashes)
   - Musculoskeletal (DDH, fractures, sports injuries)

7. **Preventive Care & Health Supervision** — ~10%
   - Well-child visit schedules
   - Anticipatory guidance by age
   - Safety counseling (SIDS, car seats, drowning, poisoning)
   - HEEADSSS assessment (adolescents)
   - Substance abuse screening (CRAFFT)
   - Pain management strategies
   - Pharmacology considerations (absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination)

- 2-4-6-9-12 month milestones (memorize cold)
- Red flags by age (when to refer)
- Tanner staging (first sign of puberty: girls = breast budding, boys = testicular enlargement)
- APGAR scoring and interpretation
- Developmental screening tools (ASQ-3, M-CHAT-R/F, Vanderbilt)

#### Infectious Diseases
- Fever in neonate (<28 days) = sepsis workup + admit (100% testable)
- Viral exanthems (measles vs rubella vs roseola vs fifth disease)
- Croup management (dexamethasone 0.6 mg/kg × 1)
- Bronchiolitis = supportive ONLY (no bronchodilators, no steroids)
- Immunization schedule and contraindications

#### Mental Health
- ADHD DSM-5 criteria: 6-12-2 rule
- Depression screening: PHQ-2 → PHQ-9
- Autism red flags and M-CHAT screening
- Medication management (stimulants first-line for ADHD ≥6 yo)

#### Safety & Prevention
- SIDS prevention (back to sleep, no blankets/pillows)
- Lead screening (9-12 months, 24 months)
- Child abuse recognition (TEN-4 rule for bruising)
- Mandatory reporting requirements
