🧒 D119: Developmental Milestones

Quick Reference for Pediatric Primary Care

🎯 Master Mnemonic: "2-4-6-9-12"

2 MONTHS: Social Starter (social smile, coos, tracks past midline)

4 MONTHS: Rolling Ready (laughs, reaches, rolls front-to-back)

6 MONTHS: Sitting & Solids (sits with support, babbles, start solids)

9 MONTHS: Pincer & Peek-a-Boo (pincer grasp, stranger anxiety, pulls to stand)

12 MONTHS: Walking & Words (walks/cruises, 1-2 words specific, drinks from cup)

📊 Language Development: 8 Weeks to 5 Years
Age Receptive Expressive
8 weeks Alerts to sound Coos
4 months Turns to voice Laughs, squeals
6 months Responds to name Babbles ("ba-ba")
8-10 months Understands "no" Imitates sounds
10-12 months Follows 1-step command "Mama/dada" specific
12-13 months Points to body parts 1-2 words
18-20 months Identifies pictures 10-20 words, points to wants
24 months 2-step unrelated commands 50 words, 2-word phrases
3 years Follows 3-step commands 3-word sentences, 75% intelligible
4 years Understands prepositions Tells stories, 100% intelligible
5 years Follows complex instructions Full sentences, asks "why?"

🚨 RED FLAGS by Age - "STOP & REFER"
2 MONTHS:
  • No social smile
  • Not tracking objects
4 MONTHS:
  • No cooing/laughing
  • Not reaching for objects
  • Persistent head lag
6 MONTHS:
  • No babbling
  • Not sitting with support
  • No interest in toys
9 MONTHS:
  • No stranger anxiety
  • No pincer grasp
  • Not bearing weight on legs
12 MONTHS:
  • Not walking or cruising
  • No words ("mama/dada")
  • No pointing or gestures
18 MONTHS:
  • <6 words
  • Not walking well
  • No interest in others
24 MONTHS: ⚠️ MOST CRITICAL
  • <50 words
  • No 2-word phrases
  • Not running
Most Important Red Flag: No 2-word phrases by 24 months. This is the single most predictive milestone for language delay.
Regression at ANY Age: Loss of ANY previously acquired language or social skills at ANY age requires immediate referral for ASD evaluation.

🎯 Tanner Staging Mnemonic

"Hair Grows, Breasts Show, Boys Below"

GIRLS (First sign = Breast budding): Stage 2 thelarche, age 8-13

BOYS (First sign = Testicular enlargement): Stage 2 testes >4mL, age 9-14

Menarche: Typically Tanner 3-4, average age 12-13
