PCOS vs Endometriosis

Comparative Study Guide for D120

PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome

🔬 Pathophysiology

  • Hormonal imbalance (↑ androgens)
  • Insulin resistance common
  • Multiple follicular cysts on ovaries
  • Anovulation or irregular ovulation

📋 Key Symptoms

  • Irregular/absent periods
  • Hirsutism (excess hair growth)
  • Acne
  • Weight gain/difficulty losing weight
  • Male-pattern baldness
  • Dark skin patches (acanthosis nigricans)

🩺 Diagnosis

  • Rotterdam criteria (2 of 3):
    • Oligo/anovulation
    • Clinical/biochemical hyperandrogenism
    • Polycystic ovaries on ultrasound
  • Lab: ↑ testosterone, ↑ LH/FSH ratio
  • Fasting glucose/insulin levels

💊 Treatment

  • Lifestyle: weight loss, exercise
  • OCPs (oral contraceptives) for cycle regulation
  • Metformin for insulin resistance
  • Anti-androgens (spironolactone)
  • Fertility treatments if pregnancy desired

⚠️ Complications

  • Type 2 diabetes
  • Infertility
  • Metabolic syndrome
  • Endometrial cancer risk
  • Cardiovascular disease

Endometriosis

Endometrial Tissue Outside Uterus

🔬 Pathophysiology

  • Endometrial tissue grows outside uterus
  • Responds to hormonal cycles
  • Causes inflammation & scarring
  • Adhesions form between organs

📋 Key Symptoms

  • Severe dysmenorrhea (painful periods)
  • Chronic pelvic pain
  • Dyspareunia (painful intercourse)
  • Heavy menstrual bleeding
  • Painful bowel movements/urination during period
  • Infertility

🩺 Diagnosis

  • Clinical presentation & history
  • Pelvic exam (tender nodules)
  • Imaging: ultrasound, MRI
  • Definitive: laparoscopy with biopsy
  • Staging (I-IV based on severity)

💊 Treatment

  • NSAIDs for pain management
  • Hormonal therapy: OCPs, progestins
  • GnRH agonists (suppress estrogen)
  • Surgical: laparoscopic excision/ablation
  • Hysterectomy (severe cases)

⚠️ Complications

  • Infertility (30-50% of patients)
  • Chronic pain syndrome
  • Ovarian cysts (endometriomas)
  • Bowel/bladder dysfunction
  • Increased ovarian cancer risk

🎯 Key Differentiators

Primary Complaint

PCOS: Irregular periods, infertility, cosmetic concerns (hirsutism, acne)

Endo: Severe pelvic pain, especially with menses

Pain Pattern

PCOS: Usually painless

Endo: Cyclical severe pain is hallmark

Metabolic Issues

PCOS: Insulin resistance, weight gain, diabetes risk

Endo: No metabolic association

Hormonal Pattern

PCOS: Elevated androgens (testosterone)

Endo: Normal hormone levels

Diagnosis Method

PCOS: Clinical + labs + ultrasound

Endo: Gold standard = laparoscopy

Age of Onset

PCOS: Often presents in adolescence/early 20s

Endo: Peak diagnosis 25-35 years
