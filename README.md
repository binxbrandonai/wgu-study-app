## THE 20-MINUTE SPRINT METHOD (ADHD-Optimized)

### Why It Works
Traditional study methods fail because they demand unrealistic focus for hours. Your brain works better in short, intense bursts.

### How to Use It
1. ⏱️ **Set timer for 20 minutes**
2. 🎯 **Pick ONE section** (e.g., "Vaginitis" or "COPD")
3. 🔊 **Read aloud** or use text-to-speech (helps focus)
4. ✍️ **Write mnemonics** in your own words
5. 🏃 **Take 5-minute movement break** (walk, stretch, water)
6. 🔄 **Repeat** with next section

### Sample Study Session (60 minutes)
- **0-20 min:** Read D120 Women's Health - Vaginitis section
- **20-25 min:** Break (walk outside)
- **25-45 min:** Take 10 quiz questions on vaginitis
- **45-50 min:** Break (snack, water)
- **50-60 min:** Review visual aid - contraception decision tree

**Result:** You just studied 40 minutes effectively instead of "studying" for 3 hours while your brain wandered.

## STUDY TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### General Strategy
✅ **Quiz often** - Active recall beats passive reading every time

✅ **Study same time daily** - Build a habit (mornings work best for most people)

✅ **Eliminate distractions** - Phone in another room, notifications off, close extra tabs

✅ **Move during breaks** - Walking boosts memory retention

✅ **Don't cram** - Distributed practice (1 hr/day for 2 weeks) beats marathon sessions (7 hrs in 1 day)

✅ **Teach it to explain it** - If you can teach a concept to someone else, you understand it

### ADHD-Specific Tips
✅ **Body doubling** - Study with another person (even on video call, even if they're doing something else)

✅ **Pomodoro timers** - Use a physical timer or app like Forest, Focus Keeper

✅ **Multiple formats** - Read it, write it, hear it (NotebookLM audio), see it (visuals)

✅ **Accountability** - Tell someone your study goals for the day

✅ **Reward system** - After 3 sprints, watch 1 YouTube video / get coffee / pet your dog

✅ **Energy-based scheduling** - Study when YOUR brain works best (not when you "should")

### Test-Taking Tips
✅ **Read carefully** - WGU OAs love "Which is the FIRST step?" and "Which is CONTRAINDICATED?"

✅ **Eliminate wrong answers** - Cross out obviously wrong choices first

✅ **Flag and move on** - Don't get stuck on one question for 5 minutes

✅ **Trust your gut** - Your first instinct is usually right (don't second-guess unless you're certain)

✅ **Manage your time** - 60 questions in 90 minutes = 1.5 min per question (you'll have plenty of time)
