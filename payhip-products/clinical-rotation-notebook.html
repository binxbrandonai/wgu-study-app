📌 Using This Notebook

Print one patient log per patient encounter, one daily summary per clinical day. At the end of each rotation, tally your numbers for your clinical log.

  • Every patient you see — get at least their age, dx, and one learning point
  • Every procedure — log the date and whether you observed vs performed
  • Your preceptor signs — keep a signature page for verification
  • Reflect daily — what'd you learn, what'll you do differently
