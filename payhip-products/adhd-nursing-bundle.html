  • ✅ Low-distraction layout — clean sections, minimal visual noise
  • ✅ High-contrast color coding — amber highlights for key info, teal for section breaks

  • ✅ Printable and usable — write on these pages, check things off, make them yours
  • ✅ Imperfect action > perfect inaction — done is better than perfect

📋 Study Strategies for the ADHD Brain

Your brain works differently — work with it, not against it. These strategies are clinically proven and ADHD-specific.

⚡ Hyperfocus Leverage

When you're locked in, stay there. Don't break focus for a 'scheduled break' if you're in the zone. Ride the wave.

Tip: Keep water + snacks nearby so you don't have to get up.

🎯 5-Minute Rule

Tell yourself you'll study for just 5 minutes. That's it. Usually you'll keep going. The hardest part is starting.

Tip: Set a 5-min timer. When it goes off, decide if you keep going.

🔊 Dual Coding

Read it + say it out loud + draw it. Engaging multiple senses makes information stick for ADHD brains.

Tip: Use a whiteboard or scrap paper to sketch concepts while you read.

🏆 Reward Stacking

Finish a section → get a reward (snack, scroll, walk). Don't reward before the work is done.

Tip: Keep rewards small and immediate. Dopamine now, not "later."
