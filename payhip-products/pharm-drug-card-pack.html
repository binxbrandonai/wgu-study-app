❤️ Cardiovascular

Lisinopril
ACE Inhibitor
Mech:Blocks ACE → ↓ Angiotensin II → vasodilation
Use:HTN, HF, post-MI, diabetic nephropathy
Dose:10-40mg PO q24h
SE:Dry cough, ↑K+, angioedema, hypotension
Nurse:Check K+ and Cr before starting. Hold if K+ >5.5. Avoid in pregnancy.
⚠️Pregnancy Category D — fetal injury
Metoprolol
Beta-Blocker (Cardioselective)
Mech:Blocks β1 receptors → ↓ HR, ↓ contractility
Use:HTN, angina, HF, post-MI, SVT
Dose:25-400mg PO q24h (ER) or BID (IR)
SE:Bradycardia, fatigue, dizziness, masking hypoglycemia
Nurse:Check HR before giving. Do not stop abruptly (rebound HTN).
Amlodipine
Calcium Channel Blocker (DHP)
Mech:Blocks Ca channels in VSMC → vasodilation
Use:HTN, angina, Raynaud's
Dose:2.5-10mg PO q24h
SE:Peripheral edema, headache, dizziness, flushing
Nurse:Monitor BP, assess for ankle swelling. Grapefruit juice ↑ levels.
Furosemide
Loop Diuretic
Mech:Blocks Na-K-2Cl in loop of Henle
Use:HF, pulmonary edema, renal disease, HTN
Dose:20-80mg PO/IV, may titrate up
SE:Hypokalemia, hyponatremia, dehydration, ototoxicity
Nurse:Monitor I/O, daily weight, K+ levels. Give early AM to avoid nocturia.
Warfarin
Vitamin K Antagonist
Mech:Inhibits vitamin K-dependent clotting factors II, VII, IX, X
Use:Atrial fib, DVT/PE, mechanical valves
Dose:2-10mg PO q24h, titrate to INR 2-3
SE:Bleeding, bruising, skin necrosis
Nurse:Monitor INR. Hold if INR > therapeutic. Vitamin K = antidote. Many drug interactions.
⚠️Risk of major or fatal bleeding
Atorvastatin
HMG-CoA Reductase Inhibitor
Mech:Inhibits cholesterol synthesis in liver
Use:Hyperlipidemia, CAD risk reduction
Dose:10-80mg PO q24h
SE:Myalgia, ↑LFTs, rhabdomyolysis (rare)
Nurse:Monitor LFTs and CK if muscle pain. Give at any time of day. Avoid with grapefruit.

🦠 Antibiotics

Amoxicillin
Aminopenicillin
Mech:Inhibits cell wall synthesis
Use:CAP, sinusitis, AOM, strep pharyngitis
Dose:500mg-1g PO TID or 875mg BID
SE:Rash, diarrhea, C diff, allergic rxns
Nurse:Ask about PCN allergy before giving. Take with food if GI upset.
Ceftriaxone
3rd-Gen Cephalosporin
Mech:Inhibits cell wall synthesis
Use:CAP, pyelonephritis, meningitis, gonorrhea
Dose:1-2g IV/IM q24h
SE:Diarrhea, rash, ↑LFTs, biliary sludge
Nurse:Do not give with calcium (precipitates). Cross-allergy with PCN ~10%.
Vancomycin
Glycopeptide
Mech:Inhibits cell wall synthesis
Use:MRSA, C diff (PO), endocarditis, osteomyelitis
Dose:15-20mg/kg IV q8-12h
SE:Red man syndrome, nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity
Nurse:Check trough levels (goal 15-20). Infuse over 60+ min. PO for C diff only.
Azithromycin
Macrolide
Mech:Binds 50S ribosomal subunit → inhibits protein synthesis
Use:CAP, pertussis, chlamydia, MAC prophylaxis
Dose:500mg PO x1 then 250mg x4d
SE:GI upset, QT prolongation, C diff
Nurse:Can prolong QT interval — check EKG if on other QT-prolonging meds.
TMP-SMX
Sulfonamide Combo
Mech:Inhibits folate synthesis (sequential blockade)
Use:UTI, MRSA skin, PCP prophylaxis, Shigella
Dose:DS 1 tab PO BID
SE:Rash, photosensitivity, ↑K+, Stevens-Johnson
Nurse:Sulfa allergy = contraindicated. Encourage fluid intake to prevent crystals.
⚠️Fatal reactions including SJS
Ciprofloxacin
Fluoroquinolone
Mech:Inhibits DNA gyrase / topoisomerase IV
Use:UTI, pyelonephritis, prostatitis, pseudomonas
Dose:250-750mg PO BID
SE:Tendon rupture, QT prolongation, C diff, neuropathy
Nurse:NOT first-line for uncomplicated UTI. Avoid in <18 and >60. Tendon warning.
⚠️Tendonitis/tendon rupture risk

🧠 CNS

Sertraline
SSRI
Mech:Blocks serotonin reuptake → ↑5-HT
Use:Depression, anxiety, OCD, PTSD, PMDD
Dose:50-200mg PO q24h
SE:Nausea, insomnia, sexual dysfunction, weight gain
Nurse:4-6 wks for full effect. Do not stop abruptly (discontinuation syndrome). Serotonin syndrome risk.
⚠️↑ suicidal ideation in young adults first few weeks
Lorazepam
Benzodiazepine
Mech:Potentiates GABA-A receptor
Use:Anxiety, seizures, alcohol withdrawal, sedation
Dose:0.5-2mg PO/IV q4-8h PRN
SE:Sedation, ataxia, dependence, respiratory depression
Nurse:Fall risk. Avoid with alcohol. Taper to discontinue. Flumazenil = reversal agent.
Methylphenidate
CNS Stimulant
Mech:Blocks DA/NE reuptake → ↑ focus
Use:ADHD, narcolepsy
Dose:10-60mg PO q24h (ER) or BID-TID (IR)
SE:↓ appetite, insomnia, ↑HR, HA, growth delay
Nurse:Monitor growth in children. Give early to avoid insomnia. CII controlled substance.
⚠️High abuse potential, monitor for misuse

🫁 Respiratory

Albuterol
SABA (β2-Agonist)
Mech:Stimulates β2 receptors → bronchodilation
Use:Asthma, COPD exacerbation
Dose:2.5mg NEB or 2 puffs MDI q4-6h PRN
SE:Tachycardia, tremor, anxiety, ↑ glucose
Nurse:Check HR before and after. Assess lung sounds. Overuse = poor asthma control.
Fluticasone
ICS (Inhaled Corticosteroid)
Mech:Anti-inflammatory in airways
Use:Asthma controller, COPD maintenance
Dose:88-880mcg INH BID dependent on device
SE:Oral thrush, dysphonia, cough
Nurse:Rinse mouth after use to prevent thrush. Not for acute attacks. Use daily.
Prednisone
Systemic Corticosteroid
Mech:Suppresses inflammation and immune response
Use:Asthma/COPD exacerbation, autoimmune, allergic rxn
Dose:10-60mg PO q24h, taper required if >2 weeks
SE:↑ glucose, HTN, osteoporosis, weight gain, insomnia, immunosuppression
Nurse:Monitor glucose. Taper to prevent adrenal insufficiency. Avoid live vaccines.

🫘 Endocrine

Insulin Lispro
Rapid-Acting Insulin
Onset:15 min | Peak: 1h | Duration: 3-4h
Use:Prandial/meal-time coverage, sliding scale
Dose:Per sliding scale or insulin-to-carb ratio
SE:Hypoglycemia, weight gain, lipodystrophy
Nurse:Give within 15 min of meal. Do not give if meal skipped. Clear = rapid.
Metformin
Biguanide
Mech:↓ hepatic glucose production, ↑ insulin sensitivity
Use:Type 2 DM (first-line), prediabetes
Dose:500-2000mg PO q24h (ER) or BID (IR)
SE:GI upset, diarrhea, metallic taste, B12 deficiency
Nurse:Hold for IV contrast + 48h (lactic acidosis risk). Take with food. Monitor B12.
⚠️Lactic acidosis risk with renal impairment
Levothyroxine
Thyroid Hormone (T4)
Mech:Replaces endogenous thyroid hormone
Use:Hypothyroidism, post-thyroidectomy
Dose:25-200mcg PO q24h, titrate to TSH
SE:Overdose = hyperthyroid symptoms (tachycardia, weight loss, anxiety)
Nurse:Give on empty stomach 30-60min before breakfast. Do not give with calcium/iron.
