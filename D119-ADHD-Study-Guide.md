## 🧠 MASTER MNEMONIC LIST

### **APGAR Score: "APGAR = A Perfect Grading After Resuscitation"**
- **A**ppearance (Color): 0=blue, 1=body pink/extremities blue, 2=all pink
- **P**ulse: 0=absent, 1=<100, 2=>100
- **G**rimace (Reflex): 0=no response, 1=grimace, 2=cry/cough/sneeze
- **A**ctivity (Tone): 0=limp, 1=some flexion, 2=active motion
- **R**espiration: 0=absent, 1=weak cry, 2=strong cry

**Scoring:**
- 7-10 = Normal (most newborns)
- 4-6 = Moderate distress (needs intervention)
- 0-3 = Severe distress (immediate resuscitation)
- Scored at **1 and 5 minutes** (repeat at 10 if <7 at 5 min)

### **Newborn Reflexes: "SMART BABY"**
- **S**ucking (birth-4 months)
- **M**oro (startle, birth-4 months)
- **A**symmetric tonic neck "fencing" (birth-4 months)
- **R**ooting (birth-4 months)
- **T**onic neck (birth-6 months)

- **B**abinski (birth-12 months, up-going toe)
- **A**bdominal (appears at 1 year, stays lifelong)
- **B**arlow/Ortolani (DDH screening, birth-3 months)
- **Y**awn/gag (lifelong)

### **DDH Screening: "BARLOW pushes Back, ORTOLANI returns Out"**
- **BARLOW**: Attempt to **dislocate** hip posteriorly (push back)
- **ORTOLANI**: Attempt to **reduce** a dislocated hip (bring out)
- **+** test = **CLUNK** sensation
- Screening: All newborns at birth, 2 weeks, 2 months, 6 months, 9 months

### **Developmental Milestones: "2-4-6-9-12"**

**2 MONTHS** - "Social Starter"
- Social smile ✓
- Coos
- Tracks past midline
- Lifts head 45° prone

**4 MONTHS** - "Rolling Ready"
- Laughs
- Reaches for objects
- Rolls front to back
- Head steady when sitting

**6 MONTHS** - "Sitting & Solids"
- Sits with support
- Babbles
- Transfers objects hand-to-hand
- Start solid foods (purees)

**9 MONTHS** - "Pincer & Peek-a-Boo"
- Pincer grasp (thumb + index finger)
- Stranger anxiety
- Pulls to stand
- Says "mama/dada" nonspecifically

**12 MONTHS** - "Walking & Words"
- Walks independently (or cruises)
- 1-2 words used specifically
- Drinks from cup
- Follows 1-step commands

### **RED FLAGS by Age: "STOP & REFER"**

**2 MONTHS:**
- No social smile
- Not tracking objects

**4 MONTHS:**
- No cooing/laughing
- Not reaching for objects
- Persistent head lag

**6 MONTHS:**
- No babbling
- Not sitting with support
- No interest in toys

**9 MONTHS:**
- No stranger anxiety
- No pincer grasp
- Not bearing weight on legs

**12 MONTHS:**
- Not walking or cruising
- No words ("mama/dada")
- No pointing or gestures

**18 MONTHS:**

- Not walking well
- No interest in others

**24 MONTHS:**
- <50 words
- No 2-word phrases
- Not running

### **Tanner Staging: "Hair Grows, Breasts Show, Boys Below"**

**GIRLS (First sign = Breast budding)**
- Stage 1: Prepubertal
- Stage 2: **Breast budding** (thelarche) — FIRST SIGN, age 8-13
- Stage 3: Breast enlargement + pubic hair
- Stage 4: Areola forms second mound
- Stage 5: Adult breast contour

**Menarche:** Typically Tanner 3-4 (average age 12-13)

**BOYS (First sign = Testicular enlargement)**
- Stage 1: Prepubertal
- Stage 2: **Testicular enlargement** >4mL — FIRST SIGN, age 9-14
- Stage 3: Penis lengthens + pubic hair
- Stage 4: Penis width increases
- Stage 5: Adult genitalia

### **ADHD DSM-5: "6-12-2 Rule"**
- **6** symptoms (5 if ≥17 years old)
- Before age **12**
- In **2+** settings (home, school, work)
- Causing impairment for **6+ months**

**Types:**
- **Combined**: 6+ inattentive AND 6+ hyperactive
- **Predominantly Inattentive**: 6+ inattentive only
- **Predominantly Hyperactive-Impulsive**: 6+ hyperactive only

**First-line meds:**
- **Stimulants**: Methylphenidate (Ritalin, Concerta) or Amphetamines (Adderall)
- **Non-stimulants**: Atomoxetine (Strattera), Guanfacine, Clonidine

### **Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD): "Social Communication & Repetitive Restricted"**

**Core Features (must have BOTH):**
1. **Social Communication Deficits**
   - Poor eye contact
   - No social reciprocity
   - Language delay or unusual speech
   - No joint attention (pointing, showing)

2. **Repetitive & Restricted Behaviors**
   - Hand-flapping, rocking
   - Insistence on sameness
   - Intense focused interests
   - Sensory sensitivities

**Screening:**
- **M-CHAT-R/F**: 16-30 months (Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers)
- **ADOS-2**: Gold standard diagnostic tool

**RED FLAGS:**
- No babbling by 12 months
- No gestures (pointing, waving) by 12 months
- No single words by 16 months
- No 2-word phrases by 24 months
- Loss of ANY language/social skills at ANY age

### **Common Pediatric Infections: "VIRAL vs BACTERIAL"**

**VIRAL (Most common):**
- **URI**: Rhinovirus, Adenovirus
- **Croup**: Parainfluenza (barking cough, stridor)
- **Bronchiolitis**: RSV (wheezing in <2 yo)
- **Hand-Foot-Mouth**: Coxsackie A
- **Roseola**: HHV-6 (high fever 3-5 days → rash when fever breaks)
- **Fifth Disease**: Parvovirus B19 ("slapped cheek")

**BACTERIAL:**
- **Strep pharyngitis**: Group A Strep (Centor criteria, rapid strep)
- **Otitis media**: S. pneumoniae, H. influenzae, M. catarrhalis
- **Pneumonia**: S. pneumoniae (most common)
- **UTI**: E. coli (80%)

### **Fever Management: "Age-Based Urgency"**

**<28 DAYS (Neonate):**
- **ANY fever** (≥38°C/100.4°F) = **SEPSIS workup + admit**
- Full workup: CBC, blood culture, urine culture, LP (CSF)
- Start empiric IV antibiotics (ampicillin + gentamicin OR cefotaxime)

**1-3 MONTHS:**
- Fever ≥38°C = **Serious bacterial infection risk**
- **Low-risk criteria** (Rochester/Philadelphia): Well-appearing, no focal infection, normal labs
- If low-risk: Outpatient with close f/u OR observe
- If high-risk: Full sepsis workup + admit

**>3 MONTHS:**
- Fever alone if well-appearing: Observe, symptomatic treatment
- Fever + ill-appearing: Evaluate for source, consider labs
- **Antipyretics**: Acetaminophen (≥3 months) or Ibuprofen (≥6 months)

### **Nutrition & Feeding: "Breast is Best, But..."**

**BREASTFEEDING:**
- **Exclusive** for 6 months (AAP/WHO recommendation)
- Continue through 12 months + as desired
- Benefits: ↓ infections, ↓ SIDS, ↓ obesity, ↓ diabetes

**CONTRAINDICATIONS:**
- Maternal HIV (in developed countries)
- Active untreated TB
- Maternal HSV lesions on breast
- Illicit drug use
- Chemotherapy
- Infant galactosemia

**FORMULA FEEDING:**
- Iron-fortified formula until 12 months
- Whole milk after 12 months (NOT before — causes anemia)

**SOLID FOODS:**
- Start at **6 months** (not before 4 months)
- Iron-fortified cereal, pureed fruits/veggies
- Advance textures by 9-12 months
- **Honey**: NOT until 12 months (botulism risk)
- **Choking hazards**: Hot dogs, grapes, popcorn, nuts (avoid <4 yo)

### **Lead Screening: "9-12 Month Universal + High Risk"**

**Universal screening:** 9-12 months AND 24 months (or per state requirements)

**High-risk factors:**
- Lives in pre-1978 housing
- Immigrant/refugee/adopted internationally
- Sibling/playmate with elevated lead
- Parent works with lead (construction, battery, pottery)

**Blood lead level (BLL) management:**
- **<5 mcg/dL:** Normal, no action
- **5-14 mcg/dL:** Education, remove source, recheck in 3-6 months
- **15-44 mcg/dL:** Remove source, nutritional counseling (iron, calcium, vit C), chelation if symptomatic
- **≥45 mcg/dL:** **Medical emergency** — chelation therapy (DMSA, EDTA)

**Symptoms:**
- Low levels: Often asymptomatic, developmental delay
- High levels: Abdominal pain, constipation, irritability, seizures

### **Child Abuse Recognition: "SCAN for Abuse"**

**S**uspicious injury patterns:
- Bruises on ears, neck, genitals, buttocks (TEN-4 rule: Torso, Ears, Neck, <4 years)
- Burns: Glove/stocking pattern, cigarette circular, branding
- Fractures: Spiral (twisting force), metaphyseal (shaking), ribs (squeezing)

**C**onflicting history:
- Story doesn't match injury
- Delay in seeking care
- Changing explanations

**A**busive head trauma:
- Retinal hemorrhages (shaken baby)
- Subdural hematoma
- Skull fractures (multiple, bilateral)

**N**eglect signs:
- Failure to thrive
- Poor hygiene
- Missed medical appointments
- Developmental delays from lack of stimulation

**Mandatory reporting:** All 50 states require healthcare providers to report **suspected** abuse (don't need proof)

## 🧬 CONCEPTUALIZING HEALTHY BEHAVIORS

### **Environmental Hazards: "Lead, Tobacco, CO, Mercury"**

**LEAD:**
- Source: Pre-1978 paint, old plumbing, imported pottery/toys
- Screen: 9-12 months, 24 months
- Dx: Venous BLL (NOT capillary — high false positives)
- Tx: Remove source, chelation if ≥45 mcg/dL

**TOBACCO (Secondhand Smoke):**
- Risks: ↑ SIDS, ↑ asthma, ↑ otitis media, ↑ URI
- Screen: Ask at every visit
- Counseling: Smoke outside, quit smoking resources

**CARBON MONOXIDE (CO):**
- Source: Faulty furnace, car exhaust, generators indoors
- Symptoms: Headache, nausea, confusion, "flu-like" (whole family sick)
- Dx: Carboxyhemoglobin level
- Tx: Remove from source, 100% O2

**MERCURY:**
- Source: Fish (tilefish, shark, swordfish, king mackerel)
- Limit: 2 servings/week of low-mercury fish (salmon, tilapia, shrimp)
- Pregnant/breastfeeding: Avoid high-mercury fish

### **Injury Prevention by Age**

**INFANTS (0-12 months):**
- **SIDS prevention:** Back to sleep, firm mattress, no blankets/pillows, room-sharing (not bed-sharing)
- **Car seat:** Rear-facing until 2 years (or max weight)
- **Falls:** Never leave on elevated surface
- **Burns:** Water heater <120°F
- **Choking:** No honey before 12 months

**TODDLERS (1-3 years):**
- **Poisoning:** Lock cabinets, poison control 1-800-222-1222
- **Drowning:** Never leave alone near water, 4-sided pool fence
- **Falls:** Window guards, stair gates
- **Car seat:** Rear-facing until 2, then forward-facing with harness

**PRESCHOOL (3-5 years):**
- **Pedestrian safety:** Hold hand near streets
- **Bicycle:** Helmet always
- **Stranger danger:** Teach name, address, phone

**SCHOOL-AGE (6-12 years):**
- **Sports:** Helmets, protective gear
- **Water:** Swim lessons, life jackets
- **Fire safety:** Escape plan, smoke detectors

**ADOLESCENTS:**
- **Motor vehicle:** Seatbelts, graduated driver licensing, no texting
- **Substance use:** Screen at every visit (CRAFFT tool)
- **Suicide:** Screen for depression (PHQ-9A), firearms in home

## 🧠 MENTAL HEALTH & BEHAVIORAL DISORDERS

### **Depression Screening: "PHQ-2 then PHQ-9"**

**PHQ-2** (all adolescents ≥12 yo annually):
1. Little interest or pleasure in doing things?
2. Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless?

**Positive screen:** ≥3/6 → Do full **PHQ-9** (or PHQ-9A for adolescents)

**PHQ-9A scoring:**
- 0-4: Minimal
- 5-9: Mild
- 10-14: Moderate → Consider therapy
- 15-19: Moderately severe → Therapy + meds
- ≥20: Severe → Urgent psych referral

**Suicidal ideation:** ANY endorsement of question 9 → **Immediate safety assessment**

**First-line meds (FDA-approved for adolescent depression):**
- **Fluoxetine (Prozac):** Age ≥8
- **Escitalopram (Lexapro):** Age ≥12

**Black box warning:** SSRIs may ↑ suicidal thoughts in <25 yo — monitor closely

### **Anxiety Disorders**

**Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD):**
- Excessive worry ≥6 months, hard to control
- Restlessness, fatigue, difficulty concentrating, irritability, muscle tension, sleep disturbance
- Screen: **SCARED** (Screen for Child Anxiety Related Disorders)
- Tx: CBT first-line, SSRIs if severe

**Separation Anxiety Disorder (SAD):**
- Excessive fear of separation from attachment figures
- **>4 weeks** in children, **>6 months** in adults
- Common age: 18 months - 3 years (stranger anxiety normal)
- Tx: CBT, parent training

**Social Anxiety:**
- Fear of social/performance situations
- Tx: CBT, SSRIs

**Screen:** STAIC (State-Trait Anxiety Inventory for Children)

### **ADHD Management**

**Behavioral therapy:**
- First-line for preschool age (<6 yo)
- Parent training, classroom interventions

**Medications (≥6 yo):**

**STIMULANTS** (First-line):
- **Methylphenidate (Ritalin, Concerta)**
  - Short-acting: 3-4 hours
  - Long-acting: 8-12 hours
- **Amphetamines (Adderall, Vyvanse)**
  - Similar duration options

**Side effects:** ↓ appetite, insomnia, headache, stomach ache, tics (↑ or unmask)
**Monitor:** Growth (height/weight), BP/HR, sleep, mood

**NON-STIMULANTS** (if stimulants fail or contraindicated):
- **Atomoxetine (Strattera):** Takes 4-6 weeks, less abuse potential
- **Guanfacine XR (Intuniv):** Alpha-2 agonist, helps hyperactivity
- **Clonidine:** Alpha-2 agonist, sedating

**Assessment tools:**
- **Vanderbilt ADHD Rating Scale** (parent + teacher)
- **Conners Rating Scale**

### **Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)**

**Screening:**
- **M-CHAT-R/F:** 16-30 months (all well-child visits)
- **Positive screen:** Immediate referral for diagnostic eval (ADOS-2)

**Interventions:**
- **Applied Behavioral Analysis (ABA):** Evidence-based, intensive
- Speech therapy, occupational therapy
- Special education services (IEP)

**NO curative medications** — medications only for co-occurring symptoms (irritability, aggression):
- **Risperidone (Risperdal):** FDA-approved for ASD irritability age ≥5
- **Aripiprazole (Abilify):** FDA-approved for ASD irritability age ≥6

### **Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD)**

**Criteria:**
- Angry/irritable mood, argumentative/defiant behavior, or vindictiveness
- ≥6 months, causing impairment
- NOT better explained by another disorder

**Tx:** Parent Management Training (PMT), family therapy

### **Conduct Disorder**

**Criteria:**
- Aggression to people/animals
- Destruction of property
- Deceitfulness/theft
- Serious rule violations
- ≥12 months (or 3+ in 6 months)

**Tx:** Family therapy, multisystemic therapy, CBT

## 🦠 INFECTIOUS DISEASES

### **Respiratory Infections**

**CROUP (Laryngotracheobronchitis):**
- **Cause:** Parainfluenza virus (most common)
- **Age:** 6 months - 3 years
- **Symptoms:** Barking cough, stridor (inspiratory), worse at night
- **Steeple sign** on x-ray (subglottic narrowing)
- **Tx:**
  - Mild: Cool mist, hydration
  - Moderate: **Dexamethasone 0.6 mg/kg PO/IM × 1** (or prednisolone 1-2 mg/kg)
  - Severe: Nebulized **racemic epinephrine** (observe 2-3 hours for rebound)

**BRONCHIOLITIS:**
- **Cause:** RSV (most common), also rhinovirus, influenza
- **Age:** <2 years (peak 2-6 months)
- **Symptoms:** Wheezing, tachypnea, retractions, crackles
- **Dx:** Clinical (viral testing NOT needed unless hospitalized)
- **Tx:** **SUPPORTIVE ONLY**
  - Hydration, nasal suctioning, oxygen if needed
  - **NO bronchodilators** (not effective)
  - **NO steroids** (not effective)
  - **NO antibiotics** (unless bacterial superinfection)
- **Prevention:** Palivizumab (Synagis) for high-risk (premature, chronic lung/heart disease)

**PNEUMONIA:**
- **Bacterial:** S. pneumoniae (most common), S. aureus (post-influenza), Mycoplasma (school-age)
- **Viral:** RSV, influenza, adenovirus
- **Symptoms:** Fever, cough, tachypnea, retractions, decreased breath sounds or crackles
- **Dx:** CXR (consolidation), blood/sputum culture if severe
- **Tx:**
  - **<5 yo:** Amoxicillin 90 mg/kg/day (covers S. pneumoniae)
  - **≥5 yo:** Amoxicillin OR azithromycin (if atypical suspected)
  - Severe: Ceftriaxone IV

**STREP PHARYNGITIS:**
- **Cause:** Group A Streptococcus (GAS)
- **Centor Criteria** (1 point each):
  - Fever
  - Tonsillar exudates
  - Tender anterior cervical lymphadenopathy
  - Absence of cough
- **Score 3-4:** Do rapid strep test
- **Positive rapid strep OR positive culture:** Treat
- **Tx:** Amoxicillin 50 mg/kg/day × 10 days (or Pen VK)
  - PCN allergy: Azithromycin × 5 days or Cephalexin × 10 days
- **Complications:** Rheumatic fever (2-3 weeks post), Post-strep glomerulonephritis (1-2 weeks post)

### **Exanthems (Rash Illnesses)**

**MEASLES (Rubeola):**
- **Cause:** Paramyxovirus
- **Symptoms:**
  - **3 C's:** Cough, Coryza, Conjunctivitis
  - **Koplik spots:** White spots on buccal mucosa (pathognomonic)
  - **Rash:** Starts face → spreads down, maculopapular
- **Tx:** Supportive, Vitamin A
- **Prevention:** MMR vaccine × 2

**RUBELLA (German Measles):**
- **Symptoms:** Mild fever, **postauricular lymphadenopathy**, maculopapular rash (face → down)
- **Complication:** Congenital rubella (if infected in pregnancy)
- **Tx:** Supportive
- **Prevention:** MMR vaccine

**ROSEOLA (Sixth Disease):**
- **Cause:** HHV-6
- **Age:** 6 months - 2 years
- **Symptoms:** **High fever 3-5 days → rash when fever breaks**
- Rash: Pink maculopapular on trunk → spreads
- **Tx:** Supportive

**FIFTH DISEASE (Erythema Infectiosum):**
- **Cause:** Parvovirus B19
- **Symptoms:** "Slapped cheek" rash → lacy reticular rash on body
- **Complication:** Aplastic crisis in sickle cell
- **Tx:** Supportive

**VARICELLA (Chickenpox):**
- **Cause:** VZV (Varicella-Zoster Virus)
- **Symptoms:** Pruritic vesicular rash in **crops** (different stages), fever
- **Complications:** Bacterial superinfection (GAS, S. aureus), pneumonia, encephalitis
- **Tx:**
  - Supportive: Calamine, oatmeal baths, antihistamines
  - **Acyclovir:** If immunocompromised, >12 yo, or started <24 hours from rash onset
- **Prevention:** Varicella vaccine × 2

**HAND-FOOT-MOUTH DISEASE:**
- **Cause:** Coxsackie A virus
- **Symptoms:** Fever, painful oral ulcers, vesicular rash on hands/feet/buttocks
- **Tx:** Supportive (lidocaine mouthwash for pain)

### **GI Infections**

**VIRAL GASTROENTERITIS:**
- **Cause:** Rotavirus (vaccine available), Norovirus
- **Symptoms:** Vomiting, diarrhea, fever, dehydration
- **Tx:** Oral rehydration therapy (Pedialyte), BRAT diet myth (feed normal diet)

**DEHYDRATION ASSESSMENT:**

| Sign | Mild (3-5%) | Moderate (6-9%) | Severe (≥10%) |
|------|-------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Mental status | Alert | Irritable | Lethargic |
| Mucous membranes | Slightly dry | Dry | Parched |
| Tears | Present | Decreased | Absent |
| Skin turgor | Normal | Tenting | Severe tenting |
| Fontanelle | Normal | Sunken | Severely sunken |
| Urine output | Slightly ↓ | ↓ | Minimal/absent |

**Tx:**
- **Mild-moderate:** Oral rehydration 50-100 mL/kg over 3-4 hours
- **Severe:** IV fluids (20 mL/kg bolus, reassess)

## 💊 IMMUNIZATIONS

### **Contraindications**

**ALL VACCINES:**
- Severe allergic reaction to prior dose or component
- Moderate-severe acute illness (defer until recovered)

**LIVE VACCINES (MMR, Varicella, Rotavirus, LAIV):**
- Pregnancy
- Immunocompromised (HIV with CD4 <15%, chemotherapy, high-dose steroids >2 weeks)
- Recent immunoglobulin (defer MMR/Varicella 3-11 months depending on dose)

**NOT contraindications:**
- Mild illness (URI, diarrhea) without fever
- Antibiotic use
- Prematurity (use chronological age, give full dose)
- Breastfeeding
- Egg allergy (MMR safe, Flu vaccine safe)

### **Catch-Up Schedules**

**Never restart series** — just continue where left off

**Minimum intervals:**
- Most vaccines: **4 weeks** between doses
- MMR/Varicella: **3 months** between doses if catch-up needed
- Hep A: **6 months** between doses

## 🩺 SYSTEM-BASED CONDITIONS (High-Yield Examples)

### **CARDIOVASCULAR**

**INNOCENT HEART MURMURS:**
- **Still's murmur:** Musical, vibratory, 3-6 yo, LSB
- **Venous hum:** Continuous, upper chest, louder upright, disappears supine
- **Pulmonary flow murmur:** Systolic ejection, upper LSB, thin/febrile children
- **ALL innocent:** Asymptomatic, normal S1/S2, no radiation, <3/6 intensity
- **Refer if:** Diastolic, loud (≥3/6), symptomatic (FTT, cyanosis, dyspnea)

### **GASTROINTESTINAL**

**CONSTIPATION:**
- **Functional:** 95% of pediatric constipation
- **Rome IV criteria:** <3 stools/week, hard stools, painful defecation
- **Tx:**
  - Increase fluids, fiber, exercise
  - **Polyethylene glycol (MiraLAX):** First-line, safe long-term
  - Avoid suppositories/enemas if possible (psychological aversion)

**INTUSSUSCEPTION:**
- **Age:** 6 months - 2 years
- **Symptoms:** Intermittent severe colicky abdominal pain, "currant jelly stool," palpable RUQ mass
- **Dx:** Ultrasound (target sign)
- **Tx:** Air or contrast enema (80% success), surgery if fails

### **GENITOURINARY**

**UTI:**
- **<2 yo:** Often no localizing symptoms (fever, irritability, poor feeding)
- **≥2 yo:** Dysuria, frequency, urgency, abdominal/back pain
- **Dx:** Urinalysis + culture (bag specimen NOT acceptable for culture — need cath or clean catch)
- **Tx:**
  - Uncomplicated: Cephalexin, Bactrim, Amoxicillin-clavulanate × 7-10 days
  - Complicated or <2 mo: Ceftriaxone IV, admit if toxic-appearing
- **Imaging:** VCUG if <2 yo with first febrile UTI (r/o vesicoureteral reflux)

### **DERMATOLOGY**

**ECZEMA (Atopic Dermatitis):**
- **Symptoms:** Pruritic, erythematous, scaly patches
  - Infants: Face, scalp, extensor surfaces
  - Children: Flexor surfaces (antecubital, popliteal)
- **Tx:**
  - **Moisturizers:** Multiple times daily (Cetaphil, Eucerin)
  - **Topical steroids:** Low-potency (hydrocortisone 1%) for face, medium-potency (triamcinolone) for body
  - **Avoid triggers:** Fragrances, harsh soaps, extreme temps
  - Severe: Topical calcineurin inhibitors (tacrolimus, pimecrolimus)

## ✅ PREVENTIVE CARE & ANTICIPATORY GUIDANCE

### **HEEADSSS Assessment (Adolescents)**

Structured interview for psychosocial screening:

- **H**ome: Living situation, relationships
- **E**ducation/Employment: School performance, future plans
- **E**ating: Diet, body image, eating disorders
- **A**ctivities: Hobbies, exercise, screen time
- **D**rugs: Tobacco, alcohol, marijuana, other substances (CRAFFT tool)
- **S**exuality: Sexual activity, contraception, STI risk
- **S**uicide/Depression: Mood, self-harm, safety (PHQ-9A)
- **S**afety: Seatbelts, helmets, violence, firearms

**Confidentiality:** Explain limits (suicidal/homicidal ideation, abuse) at start

### **CRAFFT Substance Abuse Screening (Adolescents)**

2+ "yes" = positive screen

- **C**ar: Ridden in car driven by someone high/drunk?
- **R**elax: Use alcohol/drugs to relax?
- **A**lone: Use alcohol/drugs when alone?
- **F**orget: Forget things while using?
- **F**amily/Friends: Told you to cut down?
- **T**rouble: Gotten into trouble while using?

## 🎓 FINAL EXAM TIPS

### **Time Management**
- 60 questions in 90 minutes = **1.5 minutes per question**
- Flag difficult questions, return at end
- First pass: Answer confident questions (aim for 40 min)
- Second pass: Tackle flagged questions (40 min)
- Final pass: Review all answers (10 min)

### **Strategy**
1. **Read question stem FIRST** (what are they asking?)
2. **Cover answer choices** — formulate your answer
3. **Eliminate wrong answers** — usually 2 obviously wrong
4. **Choose between remaining 2** — pick most complete/safe answer

### **Key Buzzwords**
- **"Most appropriate NEXT step"** → Think stepwise (assess before treat)
- **"Most likely diagnosis"** → Classic presentation = classic answer
- **"Best initial test"** → Least invasive, most sensitive
- **"Most accurate test"** → Gold standard (may be invasive)

### **When in Doubt**
- **Safety first** → More conservative/cautious answer
- **Age matters** → Neonates/infants are NOT small adults
- **Developmental milestones** → Memorize 2-4-6-9-12-18-24 months
- **Red flags** → Always refer/escalate if abnormal
