# NotebookLM Setup Guide - AI Study Partner

NotebookLM is Google's FREE AI-powered research assistant. It's perfect for turning these study guides into an interactive study partner.

## WHAT IS NOTEBOOKLM?

Think of it as ChatGPT but trained ONLY on YOUR study materials. It can:
- Generate audio overviews (listen while driving/walking)
- Answer questions based on the study guides
- Create practice quiz questions
- Explain complex topics conversationally
- Summarize key concepts

**Cost:** FREE  
**Website:** https://notebooklm.google.com

## SETUP (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create Account
1. Go to https://notebooklm.google.com
2. Sign in with your Google account (or create one free)
3. Click "New Notebook"

### Step 2: Upload Study Materials
1. Click "Add Source"
2. Choose "Upload files"
3. Upload these files:
   - D118-study-guide.pdf
   - D119-study-guide.pdf
   - D120-ADHD-Study-Guide.pdf
   - D120-COMPETENCY-UPDATE-SUMMARY.pdf
4. Wait 30-60 seconds for processing

### Step 3: Start Using

**Generate Audio Overview:**
- Click "Audio Overview" button
- NotebookLM creates a 10-15 minute podcast-style summary
- Listen while commuting, walking, or exercising
- Great for auditory learners!

**Ask Questions:**
- Type: "What are the USPSTF screening guidelines for colorectal cancer?"
- Type: "Compare BV, Trich, and Candida vaginitis"
- Type: "Create 10 practice questions on women's health"

**Create Study Guides:**
- Ask: "Create a one-page cheat sheet for D120 high-yield topics"
- Ask: "What are the most commonly tested concepts in geriatric care?"

## BEST PRACTICES

✅ **Upload all course materials at once** - NotebookLM works better with complete context

✅ **Ask specific questions** - "What's the first-line treatment for uncomplicated UTI in non-pregnant women?" works better than "Tell me about UTIs"

✅ **Generate multiple audio overviews** - Create one per course, or one per topic area

✅ **Use it for self-quizzing** - "Quiz me on 10 random D119 topics"

✅ **Request comparisons** - "Create a comparison table for asthma severity classifications"

## EXAMPLE PROMPTS TO TRY

**For D118 (Acute Illness):**
- "What are the red flag symptoms that require immediate referral?"
- "Create a decision tree for treating acute bronchitis vs pneumonia"
- "Quiz me on 10 EENT topics"

**For D119 (Chronic Illness):**
- "Compare HFrEF and HFpEF management"
- "What are the first-line medications for each chronic condition?"
- "Create flashcards for hypertension guidelines"

**For D120 (Special Populations):**
- "What are the highest-yield topics for women's health on the OA?"
- "Explain fall risk assessment tools for older adults"
- "Create 15 practice questions on contraception"

## LIMITATIONS

❌ NotebookLM only knows what you upload - it can't access new medical guidelines published after your materials  
❌ Always verify critical clinical information against primary sources  
❌ Use it as a study TOOL, not a replacement for understanding the material  

## PRIVACY

✅ Your study materials are private to your account  
✅ Google doesn't train AI models on your NotebookLM data  
✅ Your conversations stay within your notebook  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

**Upload failed?**
- Try uploading one file at a time
- PDFs work better than .md files
- File size limit is 500KB per file (these guides are well under)

**Audio overview not generating?**
- Wait 24 hours after first using NotebookLM (Google sometimes rate-limits new users)
- Try refreshing the page
- Make sure you have at least 2 sources uploaded

**Answers seem incorrect?**
- Remember: AI can hallucinate. Always verify against your uploaded materials.
- Ask it to cite which document it's referencing

---

**Questions?** Email [your-support-email]

**Happy studying!** 🎧🧠

## DAILY STUDY TIPS

✅ **Use the Pomodoro Technique:** 20 min focus, 5 min break  
✅ **Study Same Time Daily:** Build a habit (morning works best for most)  
✅ **Eliminate Distractions:** Phone in another room, notifications off  
✅ **Move During Breaks:** Walk, stretch, do jumping jacks  
✅ **Track Your Progress:** Check off completed sessions  
✅ **Don't Cram:** Distributed practice beats marathon sessions  
✅ **Quiz Often:** Active recall is more effective than re-reading
