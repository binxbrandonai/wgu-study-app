**Turn your study guides into an interactive AI tutor for FREE**

## WHAT IS NOTEBOOKLM?

NotebookLM is Google's **FREE** AI-powered research assistant. It's like ChatGPT, but trained ONLY on the documents YOU upload.

### What Makes It Perfect for Studying?

✅ **No hallucinations** - It only references YOUR study materials (no made-up information)

✅ **Audio overviews** - Generates 10-15 minute podcast-style summaries you can listen to while driving/walking/exercising

✅ **Conversational learning** - Ask questions in plain English, get answers based on your uploaded materials

✅ **Free forever** - No credit card, no trial period, no paid tier (as of June 2026)

✅ **Private** - Your study materials stay private to your Google account

### Perfect For:
- Auditory learners who absorb better by listening
- Commuters who want to study during drive time
- Visual learners who want concept maps generated
- Anyone who learns better through conversation than reading

## QUICK SETUP (5 MINUTES)

### Step 1: Create Account
1. Go to **https://notebooklm.google.com**
2. Sign in with your Google account (or create free account at https://accounts.google.com)
3. Click **"New Notebook"** (blue button, top right)
4. Give it a name: "WGU FNP - D118/D119/D120"

### Step 2: Upload Study Materials
1. Click **"+ Add Source"** (or just drag and drop files)
2. Choose **"Upload files"**
3. Upload these files from your study bundle:
   - `D118-study-guide.md`
   - `D119-study-guide.md`
   - `D120-ADHD-Study-Guide.md`
   - `D120-COMPETENCY-UPDATE-SUMMARY.md`
4. Wait 30-60 seconds for processing (progress bar shows upload)

**Pro Tip:** If you converted .md files to PDF, upload the PDFs instead. Both work fine!

### Step 3: Generate Your First Audio Overview
1. Click **"Audio Overview"** button (looks like a speaker icon)
2. Wait 3-5 minutes for generation (NotebookLM creates a conversation between two AI hosts discussing your materials)
3. Click **Play** and listen!

**What you'll get:** A 10-15 minute podcast-style discussion covering the main topics in your study guides. Perfect for your commute!

## HOW TO USE NOTEBOOKLM FOR STUDYING

### 1️⃣ GENERATE AUDIO OVERVIEWS (Best for Auditory Learners)

**What it does:** Creates a conversational podcast between two AI hosts discussing your study materials.

**How to use it:**
1. Upload all materials for ONE course (e.g., D118 guide)
2. Click "Audio Overview"
3. Listen during:
   - Commute to work/school

   - Cooking/cleaning
   - Before bed (passive review)

**Pro Tips:**
- Generate separate audio overviews for each course (D118, D119, D120)
- Re-generate after studying a section to reinforce learning
- Listen at 1.25x or 1.5x speed if you want faster review
- Download the MP3 to listen offline (click download icon)

**Example Results:**
- D118 overview: ~12 minutes covering USPSTF guidelines, acute illness patterns, first-line treatments
- D119 overview: ~15 minutes covering chronic disease management, drug classes, screening intervals
- D120 overview: ~14 minutes covering women's health, geriatrics, special populations

3. It cites which source document it's referencing

**Example Questions to Try:**

**For D118:**
- "What are the USPSTF screening guidelines for colorectal cancer?"

**For D119:**
- "Compare HFrEF and HFpEF - symptoms, diagnosis, and treatment"
- "What are the GOLD stages of COPD and their treatment?"
- "Explain the CURB-65 score for pneumonia"
- "What's the first-line DMARD for rheumatoid arthritis?"

**For D120:**
- "Compare BV, Trich, and Candida vaginitis"
- "What are the highest-yield topics for the D120 OA?"
- "Explain the fall risk assessment tools"
- "What contraception is safe for a 38yo smoker with migraines?"

**Pro Tips:**
- Ask specific questions (better answers than broad "tell me about...")
- Request comparisons - NotebookLM excels at side-by-side analysis

## LIMITATIONS TO KNOW

### What NotebookLM CAN'T Do:
❌ **Access new information** - It only knows what you uploaded. It won't fetch the latest USPSTF 2027 guidelines unless you upload them.

❌ **Replace critical thinking** - It can explain concepts, but you still need to apply them on the OA.

❌ **Guarantee accuracy** - While it rarely hallucinates (unlike ChatGPT), always verify critical clinical information against your primary sources.

❌ **Study for you** - You still need to put in the work. NotebookLM makes studying MORE efficient, not automatic.

### What NotebookLM CAN Do:
✅ **Explain complex topics** in simpler terms
✅ **Generate practice questions** similar to your OA
✅ **Create audio summaries** for passive review
✅ **Answer specific questions** based on your materials
✅ **Compare and contrast** related concepts
✅ **Organize information** visually (tables, lists, outlines)

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Audio overview not generating?
**Solution 1:** Wait 24 hours after first using NotebookLM (Google sometimes rate-limits new users)

**Solution 2:** Try refreshing the page

**Solution 3:** Make sure you have at least 2 sources uploaded

**Solution 4:** Check that your sources successfully uploaded (they should have green checkmarks)

---

### Answers seem wrong or incomplete?
**Solution 1:** Remember - NotebookLM only knows what you uploaded. If a topic isn't in your study guides, it can't answer.

**Solution 2:** Ask it to cite its source: "Where in my materials does it say this?"

**Solution 3:** Upload additional materials (e.g., if you have course textbook PDFs, add those)

**Solution 4:** Verify against your primary study materials - always cross-check critical info

---

### Upload failed?
**Solution 1:** Try uploading one file at a time

**Solution 2:** PDFs work better than .md files (convert using https://www.markdowntopdf.com)

**Solution 3:** File size limit is 500KB per file - your study guides are well under this, but if you add textbook PDFs, compress them first

**Solution 4:** Supported formats: PDF, .txt, .md, .docx, Google Docs, websites (paste URL)

---

### Can't find a previous conversation?
**Solution:** All conversations stay within your notebook. Scroll up in the chat panel on the right. Use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) to search your chat history.

## PRIVACY & SECURITY

### Is my data private?
✅ **Yes.** Your uploads are private to your Google account.

✅ **Google does NOT train AI models on your NotebookLM data** (this is explicitly stated in their privacy policy).

✅ Your conversations stay within your notebook - not shared with other users.

✅ Only you can access your notebook (unless you explicitly share it).

### Can I delete my data?
✅ Yes. Delete individual sources by clicking the ⋮ (three dots) next to the source name.

✅ Delete entire notebook by clicking ⋮ (three dots) at top → Delete notebook.

✅ Your data is removed from NotebookLM when deleted.
